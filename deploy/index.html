◆ Who I am

I am a cybersecurity researcher and lab engineer with an MS in Cybersecurity Management from the University of Detroit Mercy.

Most of my time goes into UDM's Center for Cyber Security and Intelligence Studies, where I build and maintain the virtual lab environments our courses run on. This past year that meant developing 36 Docker-based labs for a graduate textbook, split across three tracks: defensive operations, offensive security, and cyber governance. I write the instructions, build the environments, and test every lab end to end before it reaches a student.

Outside of the labs I compete. I took second (tie) at the ISACA Detroit competition this year as the only solo entrant to place, and captained our NCAE Cyber Games team to third in the Midwest. I also run the technical demos for our cyber club: live attack chains with Bash Bunnies, credential harvesting, and ransomware simulations, each one paired with the defense that would have caught it.

My degree is on the management side, so I care about governance as much as tooling. A lot of security problems are not really technical problems: they come down to organizational culture, incentives, and people, and the technology is just how we end up dealing with them. That is the part of the field I find most interesting.
